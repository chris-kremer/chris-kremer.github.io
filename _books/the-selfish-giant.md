## First impressions

I liked this quite a bit: a very short, fascinating story that I would recommend, even though I do not yet quite know what to make of it. Not fully understanding a story straight away can be part of its appeal.

## Christian imagery and a second chance

The Christian imagery is overt. The Giant initially sends the children away, but helping the little boy into the tree gives him a second chance to act kindly. The Christ-like child, his return, and the connection between mercy and the Giant's death are the parts I want to spend more time interpreting.

## Still thinking

I am not sure I have fully grasped the story yet. Its apparent simplicity leaves a surprising amount to come back to.
